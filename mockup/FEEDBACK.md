# 6502coin Phone UI Mockup - Feedback Guide

Open the mockup on your phone or in a browser at 375px width.

## What to look at

### Grid tab (default view)
- 16x16 colored grid with a spreading wave from the top-left corner
- Tap any cell to see a tooltip with fake stats (writes, organism type, activity)
- Play/pause button and speed slider
- Coin counter at the top incrementing over time
- Status bar at the bottom showing mining mode and tick rate

### Social tab (swipe left or tap tab)
- Your miner ID with a Copy button
- Paste a partner ID and tap Connect (or just tap Connect with empty field for demo)
- Once connected: green status dot, split-screen with both boards, and mining rate changes to 1.5x SOCIAL
- Niche toast notifications appear every 10-30 seconds while connected
- Tap Disconnect to return to solo mode

### Stats tab (swipe left again)
- Coin breakdown: solo mined, social mined, niche bonuses
- Solo decay timer (changes between "full rate" and "23h until halving" based on connection)
- Board stats: active cells, fingerprints, copy events
- Detected organisms list
- Recent blocks with timestamps

## Questions for feedback

1. **Grid clarity**: Is the grid view clear? Can you tell what's alive vs dead? Is the wave-front spreading pattern visible?

2. **Social flow**: Is the social connection flow intuitive? Would you know what to do if someone handed you a miner ID?

3. **Coin counter**: Is the coin counter at the top motivating? Is the number updating at a satisfying pace? Too fast? Too slow?

4. **Information density**: Is there too much or too little information on each screen? What would you remove? What's missing?

5. **Tab navigation**: Does swiping between tabs feel natural? Is the bottom tab bar clear?

6. **Dark theme**: Is the contrast good enough to read outdoors? Any text too dim?

7. **Touch targets**: Are buttons big enough to tap comfortably? Any misses?

8. **Solo decay**: Does the "23h until next halving" timer create urgency to connect socially, or does it feel punishing?

9. **Niche notifications**: Are the Niche toast popups noticeable? Too intrusive? Not celebratory enough?

10. **What's missing?** What would you want to see that isn't here?

11. **What's confusing?** Anything that doesn't make sense at first glance?
